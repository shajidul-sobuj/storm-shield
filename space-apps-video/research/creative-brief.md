# Storm Shield — Creative Brief & Storytelling Concept
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Video 2 (228 Seconds Target | 230s Strict Limit)**

---

## 1. Executive Vision
Produce a competition-winning, 228-second cinematic scientific documentary presentation for **Storm Shield**. The film rejects generic promotional clichés, hollow AI buzzwords, and sci-fi melodrama. Instead, it mirrors the sober, authoritative tone of a NASA Technical Readiness Review and an award-winning documentary.

The central narrative arc:
> *"When humanity journeys to Mars, Earth is a distant star, and real-time medical help is a twenty-minute radio delay away. If a solar storm erupts and an astronaut falls ill, who acts? Storm Shield is their Earth-Independent Medical Guardian — combining real space weather telemetry with personal physiological baselines to guide life-saving action, without guessing, without panic, and without hallucination."*

## 3. Creative & Stylistic Pillars

### 3.1. Visual Aesthetic
- **Swiss Clinical Aerospace Standard:** Light, clean, high-contrast user interface (`bg-slate-50`, crisp slate-900 typography, emerald/amber/rose clinical status badges).
- **Zero Fake UI / Zero "Sci-Fi Gimmicks":** No cheesy 3D spinning skulls or holographic HUDs. We feature authentic screencasts of the real working React prototype, vector anatomical blueprints, and genuine satellite proton flux charts.
- **Cinematic Framing:** Documentary establishing shots of deep space transit (Orion spacecraft / Mars transit habitat), solar coronal mass ejections, and authentic laboratory/mission control environments.

### 3.2. Voiceover & Acoustic Direction
- **Voice Profile:** Calm, authoritative, articulate, warm, and natural English narration. No monotone AI cadence; paced with emotional weight and scientific rigor.
- **Sound Design:**
  - *Opening (00:00–00:15):* Low sub-bass atmospheric drone, muffled deep-space radio static, quiet heartbeat.
  - *Problem (00:43–01:40):* Tension builds with subtle clock ticking and telemetry glitch sounds during blackout.
  - *Demo (01:40–02:37):* Crisp, responsive UI interaction sounds: clean switch clicks, procedural Web Audio acoustic siren chime, and real Web Speech read-aloud excerpts.
  - *Impact & Close (02:37–03:48):* Uplifting, propulsive orchestral-electronic hybrid score, resolving into a quiet, confident closing chord.
